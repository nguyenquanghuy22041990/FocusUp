# Roadmap

# 1. Overview

This document defines the long-term product and engineering roadmap for FocusUp.

Goals:
- guide iterative development
- prioritize sustainable growth
- prevent feature overload
- maintain architectural quality
- support portfolio-quality execution

The roadmap prioritizes:
- polished user experience
- engineering craftsmanship
- emotional design
- maintainable scalability

---

# 2. Product Vision

FocusUp aims to become:

- a calm productivity companion
- a focus-oriented habit tool
- a lightweight emotional support system
- a modern Apple-platform productivity experience

The app should help users:
- focus consistently
- avoid burnout
- build meaningful progress gradually

without:
- productivity anxiety
- overwhelming complexity
- toxic hustle culture

---

# 3. Roadmap Philosophy

The roadmap follows:
- MVP-first development
- iterative enhancement
- feature validation before expansion

Prioritize:
- depth over breadth
- polish over quantity
- consistency over experimentation

Avoid:
- feature bloat
- unnecessary complexity
- premature scaling

---
